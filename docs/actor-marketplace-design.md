# Actor marketplace storefront

Design direction (2026-10-28): a practical, premium storefront for small-business owners choosing a focused AI task. Warm ivory, dark evergreen ink and restrained Nexus gold; native light/dark themes. System sans for clear task information, Georgia display type for an editorial opening, monospace category/status labels. Wide asymmetric opening pairs a concrete business-task introduction with a visibly fictional sample report; the signature document preview links to the existing free sample. A scannable three-column catalog becomes one column on narrow phones, with contained category scrolling, search and honest empty/error states. Small category-specific line graphics replace generic decorative imagery. Borders and modest corners separate useful groups; motion is limited to card hover and respects reduced-motion settings.

Scope: marketplace storefront only. Catalog entries, costs, beta flags and tool destinations still come from the server; shared wallet, checkout, claim and refund behavior remain in their existing services. No provider/model calls or customer purchases are needed for visual validation. The sample preview repeats the existing fictional sample in research.html and is labeled illustrative. Credit packs show credits, avoiding a single search-count estimate across differently priced actors. No invented usage, reviews, revenue, speed or success claims.

## Navigation refinement

A compact introduction leads directly into the actor catalog. The single wallet sits immediately after the catalog, before supporting how-it-works content and bottom examples. Sticky Buy credits still jumps straight to it; View examples remains in the short opening. The balance, packs, optional collapsed private-link controls and APIs are unchanged. This prioritizes choosing an actor before funding the wallet on both phone and desktop.
