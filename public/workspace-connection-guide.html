NEXUS · WORKSPACE CONNECTION

Sign in. Bring your workspace.

ChatGPT uses OAuth to connect to Nexus. No API key copying, Vercel secret entry, or pod startup is needed.

  1. In ChatGPT's custom app settings, create an app named Nexus Workspace.
  2. Use this MCP server URL and choose OAuth authentication:
https://nexus-labs-sigma.vercel.app/api/workspace-mcp
  1. Use dynamic client registration. No manually created client ID or client secret is needed.
  2. Sign in to your private Nexus owner account and review the permissions.
  3. Choose Allow workspace access to finish connecting.
  4. Ask your assistant to show my Nexus board.

This connection covers Projects, the private Schedule, Reminders, and usage. Keep your private GitHub/Vercel dev connector separately for development. Customer accounts and other chat-platform callbacks are not enabled in this owner rollout.

Your earlier connection key is not used by ChatGPT OAuth. Don't paste it into chat or generate another key for this connection.
