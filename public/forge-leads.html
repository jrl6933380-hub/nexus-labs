Leads

Find businesses with no website, pick the ones you want, get a site preview and a call pitch for each. Nothing is sent, called or posted.

1 · Find businesses

Fill this in, copy the request, and give it to Claude or Qwen. They run the search on your Ally and bring back a numbered list.

2 · Pick and build

After the list comes back, type the numbers you want (like 1,3) or choose all eligible. Eligible means no website found, or only social pages, and not seen before.

3 · Use it

You get three looks for each site (Editorial, Bold, Fresh) and a call pitch you can copy. Previews are concepts with sample services, no invented reviews or prices. Confirm the business yourself before any call.
