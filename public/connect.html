Connect your Nexus Board to ChatGPT

One tap fills in the setup request. You send it, approve the connection, and your board is there.

Connect to ChatGPT

ChatGPT opens when you tap. If it asks you to sign in, sign in there. Your request will be waiting for you. You need an account that includes Plugin Creator.

What happens when you tap
  • We've already made you a Nexus account. It needs no password.
  • ChatGPT opens with the setup request filled in. Nothing is sent until you tap send.
  • ChatGPT asks to connect, then Nexus asks you to tap Allow. You can remove the connection any time in ChatGPT's settings.
