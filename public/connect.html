Connect your Nexus Board

Add your Nexus Board to ChatGPT in three steps.

What you need first

You must be signed in to ChatGPT, on an account type that includes Plugin Creator (a paid ChatGPT tool). Without that, the connection won't work.

Paste it and send

In ChatGPT, paste the prompt into the message box and tap send. When ChatGPT asks to connect, approve it, then tap Allow on the Nexus screen.

What this does
  • Creates a Nexus account for you, with no password needed yet.
  • You send the prompt yourself, so ChatGPT builds the connection only when you ask it to.
  • The Nexus screen that follows is where you approve it with Allow. You can remove the connection any time from ChatGPT's settings.
  • Your saved work stays on your account. You can add a password later to sign in from anywhere.
