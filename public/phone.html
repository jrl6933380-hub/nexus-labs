Android 11 or newer. Built for testing on your TCL ION V. Root and Shizuku are not required. Keep the phone unlocked during a session.

Checking owner access…

Enter this code in Nexus Phone within five minutes. It pairs one phone and replaces the previous pairing. Keep it private.

  1. Download and install the APK on your TCL. Android may ask you to allow installation from your browser.
  2. Sign in here as the Nexus owner. Create a pairing code and enter it in the phone app.
  3. Enable Nexus Phone control under Android Accessibility. Android 13 may also require allowing restricted settings on the app’s App info page.
  4. Tap Connect in Nexus Phone. Its persistent notification has a Disconnect button.
  5. Refresh the Nexus connector in ChatGPT/Claude to discover the phone tools. Start with phone status, then request a screen and read the result.

Prototype limits: device testing is required. Protected screens may refuse capture; password entry remains on the phone. Sessions stop on disconnect, network failure or reboot. Closing a chat does not automatically stop a connected phone. Screenshots are retained for at most two minutes; typed command text is removed from the queue when delivered. No microphone, camera, arbitrary shell, automatic startup, background AI or automatic social posting.
