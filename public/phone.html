Connect without resetting

  1. Install v0.3 on the TCL. From v0.2, choose Update: your pairing and management stay in place. No reset or uninstall needed. From v0.1, uninstall the old app first and pair again.
  2. Sign in here as the Nexus owner, create a pairing code, and enter it in Nexus Phone.
  3. Enable Nexus Phone control in Android Accessibility. If Android 13 blocks it, use App info → three-dot menu → Allow restricted settings.
  4. Tap Connect phone. The persistent notification and Nexus Home both offer Disconnect.
Dedicated phone · reset and provision

Prepare before wiping. Back up anything you need and make sure you can complete Android account verification. A reset erases the installed Nexus app and its remote connection. Initial setup must happen on the phone.

  1. Keep this page open on another screen. The QR below contains a public APK address and checksum, with no account token.
  2. Once your data and account access are ready, factory reset the TCL from Android Settings.
  3. On Android’s initial welcome screen, tap the same blank area six times to start QR provisioning, then join Wi-Fi if prompted. If that option is unavailable on this TCL build, stop and use the documented USB provisioning route.
  4. This QR installs v0.2; update to v0.3 using the download above afterward. Scan this QR. Android should download and verify Nexus Phone, then show its device-management setup. Review it and tap Finish setup.
  5. Open Nexus Phone, tap Set Nexus as Home, pair it here, enable accessibility, then Connect.

Enable deeper tools
  1. Install Shizuku from its official page. Start it using Android wireless debugging and complete its pairing on the phone.
  2. Back in Nexus Home, tap Authorize Nexus in Shizuku and approve the permission.
  3. Turn on Enable deeper tools. Brightness and force-stopping downloaded apps use Shizuku; stay-awake while charging uses device-owner management.
  4. Refresh the Nexus connector in a new ChatGPT or Claude session. Check device_info before using v0.2 commands.

Shizuku needs starting again after a non-root reboot. Remote sessions still require Connect; nothing silently starts an agent.

Recovery and control limits

Open Nexus Home → Recovery to reach Android Settings, release Nexus as Home, forget pairing, or release device management. We do not lock Settings or hide the recovery controls. Disconnect stops the remote session.

Protected screens may refuse capture, and password entry stays on the phone. A successful action can return a capture error: inspect a new screen before continuing. No unrestricted shell, remote factory reset, automatic posting, microphone or camera access. Screenshots expire after two minutes; typed command text leaves the queue when delivered.
