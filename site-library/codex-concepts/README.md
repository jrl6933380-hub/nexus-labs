# Independent Codex site concepts for Nexus ads

These are separate authored concepts, alongside Claude's original business recipes.
They are not restyles or replacements for the original sites.

## Stillmark tattoo atelier

`stillmark.fragment.html` is the exact concept Justin selected in conversation on
2026-10-20. It embeds its chosen generated moth artwork and uses optional Google
Fonts with local fallbacks. It is an editable HTML fragment for conversation
previews and later ad scene composition; do not treat it as a full document.

Design fingerprint: personal, deliberate and artistic; editorial serif headline,
comfortable body text, restrained olive/ivory material, slightly tilted artwork
plate, fine rules and a conversational consultation journey. Mobile presents text
and image in a single column; never squeeze copy next to a thumbnail. Preserve
whole words and readable body text. Each future business should have its own
structure, language, imagery and rhythm rather than inherit this look.

The displayed business is fictional. Artwork is generated concept imagery,
not a tattoo performed on a customer. The local enquiry interaction sends nothing.
Justin approved the visual direction, but browser/device QA is not complete.

Use the visualization skill's standalone export flow before distributing as a
standalone website. Preserve the source fragment for future edits. Real booking,
contact details and business claims require business-specific information.
